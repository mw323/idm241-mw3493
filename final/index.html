  • Hover OFF

RULES

Swap Button

Hover ON

  • Cursor changes to finger
  • A light gray circular background fades in behind the Swap button
  • The swap icon scales up slightly
  • The "Swap languages" label fades in and moves upward

Hover OFF

  • Cursor changes back to default
  • The light gray circular background fades out
  • The swap icon scales back to its default size
  • The label fades out and moves downward

Click

  • The circular background fades to a darker gray while the button is pressed and fades back to light gray when released
  • The swap icon rotates 180 degrees
  • The blue indicators underneath the currently active languages shrink toward the center until they disappear
  • New blue indicators expand from the center underneath the newly active languages
  • The newly active language labels transition from gray to blue
  • The current source and translated text slide to the right while fading out
  • The system swaps the languages and translation content while remembering the current swap state
  • The swapped source and translated text slide in from the left while fading in

Listen Button

Hover ON

  • Cursor changes to finger
  • A light gray circular background fades in behind the Listen button
  • The speaker icon scales up slightly
  • The "Listen" label fades in and moves upward

Hover OFF

  • Cursor changes back to default
  • The light gray circular background fades out
  • The speaker icon scales back to its default size
  • The "Listen" label fades out and moves downward

Click ON

  • The circular background fades to a darker gray while the button is pressed and fades back to light gray when released
  • The speaker icon fades out while three animated audio bars fade in
  • The "Listen" label transitions to "Stop"
  • The translation audio begins playing
  • The audio bars continuously move up and down while the audio is playing
  • Each word in the translation is highlighted in sequence, synchronized with the audio playback

Click OFF

  • The circular background fades to a darker gray while the button is pressed and fades back to light gray when released
  • The animated audio bars stop and fade out while the speaker icon fades back in
  • The "Stop" label transitions back to "Listen"
  • The translation audio stops playing
  • The current word highlight disappears and the remaining words are no longer highlighted
  • The system resets the playback state

Share Button

Hover ON

  • Cursor changes to finger
  • A light gray circular background fades in behind the Share button
  • The Share icon scales up slightly
  • The "Share" label fades in and moves upward

Hover OFF

  • Cursor changes back to default
  • The light gray circular background fades out
  • The Share icon scales back to its default size
  • The "Share" label fades out and moves downward

Click ON

  • The circular background fades to a darker gray while the button is pressed
  • The Share icon rotates and fades out while the Close icon rotates and fades in
  • The circular background fades to black and the Close icon transitions to white
  • The "Share" label fades out
  • The Email and X buttons scale up and fan out from the Share button

Click OFF

  • The black circular background fades to a darker gray while the button is pressed and fades back to light gray when released
  • The Close icon rotates and fades out while the Share icon rotates and fades back in
  • The Email and X buttons scale down and move back toward the Share button while fading out
  • The "Share" label fades back in and moves upward while the cursor remains over the button

Email Button

Hover ON

  • Cursor changes to finger
  • The circular background fades from white to light gray
  • The Email icon scales up slightly
  • The "Email" label fades in and moves upward

Hover OFF

  • Cursor changes back to default
  • The circular background fades from light gray back to white
  • The Email icon scales back to its default size
  • The label fades out and moves downward

Click

  • The Email button background fades to a darker gray while the button is pressed
  • The Email and X buttons scale down and move back toward the Share button while fading out
  • The Close icon rotates and fades out while the Share icon rotates and fades back in
  • The Share button remains black while the email application is being prepared
  • The "Opening email..." label fades in and moves upward above the Share button
  • The Share icon continuously scales up and down while the email application is being prepared
  • The system sends a request to launch the user's default email application
  • After the email application begins to open, the "Opening email..." label fades out and the Share button returns to its default state
